How to Answer Questions: The STAR Method ⭐

When responding to behavioral or scenario questions (e.g., "Describe a time when..."), structure your narrative using this framework:

S — Situation (Set the Scene) Briefly outline the context, project, or environment. (e.g., "During a group lab assignment, our team was tasked with building a working simulation within three days...")
T — Task (Your Specific Responsibility) Define your role and the specific objective or obstacle faced. (e.g., "My responsibility was to handle data calculations and verify material load limits.")
A — Action (What YOU Did) Detail the specific technical and interpersonal steps you executed. (e.g., "I tested three structural models using simulation software and recalculated stress tolerances.")
R — Result (The Outcome & Key Takeaway) Share quantifiable outcomes and lessons learned. (e.g., "Our design met all safety criteria, ranked top in our cohort, and reinforced the importance of early validation.")
