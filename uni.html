South African Bursaries

Looking for funding for your studies? Our bursaries directory helps you find the latest financial aid opportunities available to South African students at universities, TVET colleges, and private institutions.

Browse bursaries by field of study, province, or academic level, and get detailed information on eligibility, application deadlines, and how to apply.

What Are Bursaries?

Bursaries are financial awards given to students to help cover tuition fees, books, accommodation, and other study-related expenses. Most bursaries are awarded based on financial need, academic performance, or specific fields of study.
