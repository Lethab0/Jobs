National Benchmark Tests (NBTs)

Most South African universities (UCT, Wits, SU, UJ, and others) require candidates to write NBTs in Academic Literacy (AQL) and Quantitative Mathematics (MAT) alongside NSC scores.

Extended Degree Programmes (ECP)

If your APS is 2 to 4 points below direct entry minimums, extended 4-year degree programs provide additional foundational support in mathematics and science at many universities.

Matric Upgrade & TVET Pathways

Consider rewriting specific subjects through Second Chance Matric programs or enrolling in an N4–N6 Engineering Diploma at a TVET college to articulate into university.
